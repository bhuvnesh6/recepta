About Recepta

An AI receptionist installed directly on your website.

Recepta isn't just a chatbot. It's a dedicated AI team member that learns your business from your website and documents, talks to visitors in chat or natural voice, and turns conversations into qualified leads and booked appointments — automatically, around the clock.

Never miss a visitor. Answers questions and starts conversations 24/7, even while your team is offline.
Turns visitors into leads. Naturally collects names, contact details, and what they need — no rigid forms.
Books the appointment too. Checks real availability and confirms visits without back-and-forth emails.
Features

Everything a real receptionist does. None of the payroll.

Built for home services, real estate, clinics, and any business where a missed visitor is a missed customer.

Chat & Real Voice
Visitors can type or actually talk to your AI receptionist with natural, low-latency voice conversations.
Learns Your Business
Crawls your website and reads your PDFs/docs, so answers come from your real business info — never made up.
Lead Capture & Qualification
Naturally collects name, contact info, and requirements, and flags high-intent visitors as qualified leads.
Appointment Booking
Checks real availability and books appointments or site visits directly inside the conversation.
Smart Team Assignment
Routes each lead to the right person automatically — by rule, territory, or simple round robin.
Multi-language
Talks to visitors in the language they're comfortable in, no separate setup needed.
Real Analytics
See visitors, conversations, leads, and conversion rate on a live dashboard — no guessing what's working.
Fully Customizable
Your logo, your colors, your greeting, your voice — the widget looks like it belongs on your site.
