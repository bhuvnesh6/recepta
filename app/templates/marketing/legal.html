Recepta ("we", "us") provides an AI website receptionist platform ("Service") for businesses ("Organizations") and their website visitors ("Visitors"). This Privacy Policy describes, at a general level, the kinds of information collected through the Service and how it is used.

Information we collect. Account information for Organization users (name, email); business information provided by Organizations (website content, uploaded documents, agent configuration); and Visitor interaction data (chat/voice conversation transcripts, IP address, lead details a Visitor chooses to share such as name, phone, and email).
